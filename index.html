About me

I have a master's in Informatics and Computing Engineering from FEUP, and I've been passionate about front-end development and UI/UX design since my student days.

Today I work on Teamtailor's recruitment platform with Ruby on Rails, Ember and TypeScript. I like interfaces that feel calm and clear, and code that's easy for the next person to change.

Outside work I colour with alcohol markers and build small apps for things I actually use: palettes, gifts, release dates.
