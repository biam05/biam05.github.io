About me

I have a master's in Informatics and Computing Engineering from FEUP. Front-end and UI/UX design have been my thing since my student days, and what I care about most is still the experience of the person on the other side of the screen.

I've been at Teamtailor since 2023, first as a trainee and now as a full stack developer on the recruitment platform. I work with Ruby on Rails, Ember.js and TypeScript, from the design all the way to the implementation.

My interest in AI and agentic development keeps growing. I work with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex, write custom skills for them, and love trying new things. That's how my side projects start: small tools that help me at work and with my hobbies, like colouring with alcohol markers.
